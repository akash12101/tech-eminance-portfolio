What makes our web
design team different

Four things that separate us from every other web design team your business or agency has used.

Figma-Native From the First Conversation

Every project starts in Figma and stays there until design sign-off. We do not hand developers a PDF mockup and hope for the best. Our designers build with auto-layout, component variants, and responsive frames, so the handoff to development is a direct translation, not an interpretation.

Performance and Accessibility Are Non-Negotiable

Most web teams tick accessibility and performance boxes on request. We make them defaults. Lighthouse 90+ and WCAG 2.1 AA compliance are on our internal QA checklist for every project before delivery — not offered as premium add-ons. We test on real devices, not just desktop Chrome.

AI-Assisted Workflow, Human-Verified Output

Our team uses AI tools at specific points in the workflow — layout analysis, automated accessibility checks during QA, and performance diagnostics at final delivery. Every creative decision and every line of code is reviewed by people. We use AI where it catches things humans miss at speed, not to generate production shortcuts.

Transparent Scope, Honest Timelines

We tell you what a project actually requires at the briefing stage, not after the first sprint starts. If something is out of scope, we say so before starting it. We would rather have a short, direct conversation about scope than a longer, less comfortable one at invoice time.

How We Work

Our web design and
development process

Four phases, clear handoff points at each stage, and a delivery checklist that closes nothing until it passes.

Step 01

Brief and Discovery

We review your brief, brand guidelines, sitemap requirements, and competitor references. We ask the questions most teams skip — target audience, primary conversion goal, CMS editing needs, and technical constraints. A scoped brief prevents revision rounds from expanding into something nobody agreed to.

Step 02

Design and Sign-Off

Wireframes establish layout and information hierarchy before any visual design starts. High-fidelity Figma screens follow once structure is agreed — desktop, tablet, and mobile for every key template. No design goes to development until you confirm approval in writing. All feedback routes through one round.

Step 03

Development and QA

Build happens on staging against the signed-off Figma spec. Cross-browser testing, real-device mobile testing, WCAG accessibility checks, and Lighthouse audits happen at each milestone. No ticket closes without passing our internal QA checklist. You get a staging link with a written QA report attached.

Step 04

Delivery Under Your Brand

Live deployment on a pre-agreed date, managed by our team. Post-launch performance monitoring for 72 hours with immediate response to any issues, at no additional cost. Handover includes CMS documentation, component reference guides, and a Figma library with published components for future work.
